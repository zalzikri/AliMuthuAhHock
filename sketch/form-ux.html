how it works
· On create we generate a random 12-character token and store it on her record.

· The token is the page. Whoever holds the link sees her status — her, you, her buddy.

· Same model as a shared document link. Unguessable, not secret.

· Nothing sensitive is ever shown: no passwords, no employee ID, no temporary codes.

· Production path: the day her Google account is live, the page can switch to Google sign-in. The link is only needed before she has an identity.
